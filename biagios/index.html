What we found on biagios.ca

Plenty of demand. No single place to manage it.

Biagio’s has more ways to book than most restaurants: catering, holiday parties, the pizza trailer, Dinner + Show with Yuk Yuk’s, Dueling Pianos, Wine + Dine Thursdays and Meals to Go. Each one brings in leads differently. Here is where we see the biggest gains.

Inquiries

Six separate contact forms, each sending its own email

Catering, holiday parties, the pizza trailer, Dinner + Show, Dueling Pianos and the contact page each have their own form. Whoever opens the email first owns the follow-up, and nothing shows what is still waiting for a reply.

Every form feeds one CRM, opens a deal in the right pipeline, and goes to the right person on your team.
Phone

Calls come in when the dining room is closed

The dining room opens at 5 PM most days and is closed Mondays. Corporate planners usually call between 9 and 5, and an unanswered call often means they try the next caterer.

Missed calls get a text back within seconds, and the reply goes to Sarah or Ben the same day.
Guest data

Your guests are spread across five systems

OpenTable holds reservations, Silverpay handles takeout and gift cards, Uber Eats handles delivery, the newsletter has its own list, and catering clients live in inboxes. Nobody sees the whole guest.

One contact record per guest, showing their orders, events, notes and every message.
Repeat business

Happy clients aren’t asked to come back

Your reviews mention clients who book “two years in a row.” Today that depends on them remembering. Holiday parties, team lunches and summer trailer events are bookings that come back every year.

Each client gets an automatic reminder about ten months later to book the same date again.

GoHighLevel sits behind the website you already have. Nothing changes for your guests except faster replies. For your team, every inquiry shows up assigned, with the details already filled in.

Follows Canada’s anti-spam law (CASL)

We record consent on every contact. Marketing only goes to people you’re allowed to email: generally up to two years after a purchase or six months after an inquiry, unless they’ve opted in.

Works with the tools you already use

We’ll check what OpenTable, Silverpay and your newsletter can share, then tell you plainly what connects directly and what needs a workaround.

Respects the occasion

Funeral and celebration-of-life clients never get review requests or promotions. They get a simple, respectful thank-you instead.
