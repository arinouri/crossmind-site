Missed-call text-back

The lunch rush won’t cost you a catering job

When nobody can pick up the restaurant line, the caller gets a text within seconds. Replies land in one shared inbox alongside Facebook, Instagram, Google and email messages, so whoever is free can answer.

  • Works on your existing number through call forwarding, or a new local 613 number.
  • Different replies during service hours and after close.
  • Catering replies drop straight into the catering pipeline.
  • Everyone on the team can answer from the GoHighLevel phone app.

CASL-ready from day one

Canada’s anti-spam law sets how long you can message people: about two years after a purchase and six months after an inquiry, unless they opt in. We tag consent and timing on every contact so campaigns only go to people you’re allowed to reach.

Works with what you use

We’ll look at connecting OpenTable, your gift card platform and your newsletter through integrations or Zapier during discovery, and tell you plainly what connects cleanly and what needs a workaround.

The right tone for every event

Funeral and celebration-of-life catering is excluded from review asks and promotions automatically. Those clients get a simple, respectful thank-you instead.
