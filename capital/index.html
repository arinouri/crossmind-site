Squire's monthly plans (CAD)

Capital has rewards, gift cards and waitlist switched on. On Squire's current price list, rewards and gift cards come with Titan.

  • Likely cost: about CA$340 a month, or $4,080 a year, if Capital is on Titan. At minimum it's Executive at CA$205 a month. Shops on older plans can pay a different rate, so confirm it with the owner.
  • Plus about $1 from each client, every online booking. At 500 online bookings a month, clients pay roughly $6,000 a year in booking fees on top of their cuts.
  • Add-ons cost extra: a branded landing page is $25 a month, and the Operator AI is $99 a month.
  • Card processing is separate. Any system that takes cards pays this, so it's left out of every comparison on this page.

What a regular haircut earns per chair-hour

$45 haircut ÷ the time each barber books it for

  • Scott and Bao book 45 minutes for a haircut, the other five book 30. Same $45 price, so those two chairs earn $60 an hour on haircuts instead of $90. Worth asking whether that's on purpose.
  • Fades cost the same as a regular cut. Skin Fade is $45 like a Haircut, and Student Skin Fade is $40 like a Student Cut. Most shops charge more for a fade.
  • Specialties are scattered. Straight blade shaves: Jeffrey, Georges, James, Ankur. Designs: Jeffrey, Georges, Ankur. Long hair: only Georges. Weddings ($350): David and Ankur. Jeffrey and James don't do skin fades.
  • The menu needs a tidy-up. "Hair Cut" and "Haircut" both appear, some names have stray spaces, the Design add-on is listed as its own appointment, and the Wedding is booked as 0 minutes.
  • Clients must pick a barber first. "Any barber" is switched off, so someone who just wants the next free chair has to check all seven profiles.
  • The website doesn't match. capitalbarbershop.ca lists a Hot Towel Shave and a Mustache Trim with no prices, and neither has that name on the booking page.
