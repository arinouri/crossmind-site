What's different

Same flow. Five fixes.

No extra fees for clients

On Squire, paying by card online adds $2.99 on top of HST. We tested it twice. Here it's just the price plus HST.

Tip starts at "None"

Squire picks 15% for the client before the cut. Here they choose, or tip in the chair.

Add-ons that make sense

After a haircut you only see Beard Trim and Design, not the whole menu again.

No sign-in code

Name and phone to book. Phone number to move or cancel.

"Any professional"

First option on the page, showing the next free chair.

Phase 1 is booking only. Card payments, gift cards and rewards stay on Squire until phase 2, then he can cancel it. See the full switch plan ↓
