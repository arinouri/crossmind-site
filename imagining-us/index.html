Imagining Us

An Examination of Almost-Relationships

By Jalen Vale

For those who lived a thousand futures
with someone who was never truly theirs.

This book explores an under-examined form of intimacy, known as an almost-relationship. It is a compassionate guide that recognizes how a love that never quite began can still have an ending, and provides the means to promote healing by helping to separate what really happened from what you hoped would happen.
